# v0.26 Key-Art Render

The supplied Stretchicorn hero image is the canonical art-direction contract. Runtime graphics should preserve its hierarchy rather than chase unrelated procedural styles.

## Visual hierarchy

1. Stretchicorn is the brightest soft-white object.
2. The Living Scar is the only continuous full-spectrum object.
3. Corn uses rounded amber/gold kernels, deep green husks and expressive dark facial marks.
4. Cobtopus combines the golden kernel material with broad glossy purple limbs.
5. The world stays deep plum/violet and comparatively restrained so characters remain readable.
6. Bloom/specular light is selective. Decorative flicker is not a substitute for material detail.

## Stability contract

- No random whole-background displacement.
- No full-frame RGB split.
- Slow deterministic environmental sway only.
- Quantized restoration visual states where continuous alpha/color changes would shimmer.
- Filled masses before free-hanging strokes.
- Gameplay effects may move aggressively; far scenery should feel painted in place.
